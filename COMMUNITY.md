# Welcome to AI Data Tuner

A community for fine-tuning AI models and preparing high-quality training data. This is the place to:

- Ask questions about data preparation and pipeline configuration
- Share tips for getting the best results from your fine-tuning datasets
- Discuss model formats (Qwen, Llama2, Alpaca, ChatML, GPT JSONL, etc.)
- Get help with personality injection and data quality filtering
- Show off projects you've built with AI Data Tuner

## What can you do here?

- Introduce yourself and tell us about your project
- Ask questions about installation, configuration, or troubleshooting
- Share datasets, workflows, or best practices
- Help others by answering questions
- Report bugs or request features

To get started, comment below with an introduction and what you're working on!
